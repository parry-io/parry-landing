FAQ

Parry, in plain words

What is Parry?

Parry is the commercial AI intelligence, execution and autonomous platform for enterprise procurement. Using a proprietary, vertical procurement graph, it connects in a few clicks to the systems and repositories you define, consolidates one source of truth for each vendor, provides intelligence, automatically executes approved activities and automates tedious processes.

Which systems does Parry connect to?

Parry connects to the systems enterprises already run, including SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Priority, Workday, Coupa, Zip, Omnea, Opstream, Gmail, Microsoft Outlook, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Jira, ServiceNow, Google Drive, SharePoint, Box, DocuSign, Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, American Express, Zylo and Snowflake, plus 50 more systems.

What results do customers see?

Up to 35% less manual workload and up to 18% hard-dollar spend reduction. Case studies include a 15% cost reduction on strategic negotiations, 6% net savings from autonomous purchasing, $8.5m in price reductions from portfolio price consolidation, 13% less marketing and G&A leakage, and over $6.3m in potential savings found on $150m of OpEx.

How does Parry stay within our rules?

You set the guardrails. Parry negotiates, contracts, renews and transacts inside the policy, approval thresholds and mandate you define, with human governance around every layer: policy, approval, audit and escalation.

What is a Spend MRI?

A 30-minute review of your own spend data in which Parry maps every leak, every lever and every renewal at risk, such as unclaimed SLA credits, usage outside a commit, one product bought at different prices across business units, FX clauses ignored on invoices, and new discount thresholds within reach.

Which spend categories does Parry cover?

IT, SaaS, professional services, sales and marketing, R&D, maintenance and other OpEx categories.

How is Parry priced?

A pricing structure based on savings on spend: we win when you save. Parry is end-to-end and modular, so you pay only for the modules you choose.
